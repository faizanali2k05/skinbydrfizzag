SKIN BY DR. FIZZA G — ICON PACKAGE (ORIGINAL ARTWORK, UNCHANGED)
================================================================
Every image here is generated DIRECTLY from your original PNG pixels.
Logo shape and colors are NOT altered — only resized/cropped/centered.

These are raster PNGs (not vectors), per your request to keep the exact image.
PNGs are pixel-perfect to your original but do not scale infinitely; the sizes
provided cover all standard web/iOS/Android needs.

source/   logo-original.png (your file), logo-square / -white (centered square)
web/      favicons, manifest, apple-touch-icon, <head> snippet, full logo
favicon/  favicon.ico + PNG sizes
ios/      AppIcon.appiconset (all sizes, opaque) + Contents.json
android/  mipmap-* densities, adaptive icon XML, Play Store 512

PLACEMENT
web:     copy web/* to site root, paste web/head-snippet.html into <head>
ios:     drop ios/ into Assets.xcassets > AppIcon.appiconset
android: copy android/mipmap-* and res XML into app/src/main/res/

NOTE: A horizontal logo like this becomes tiny/unreadable at small icon sizes
(16-48px) because the whole lockup is squeezed into a square. That's unavoidable
without a compact symbol — but nothing about your artwork was changed.
